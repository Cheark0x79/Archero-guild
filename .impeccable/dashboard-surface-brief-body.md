## Scope and visitor mode

Guild-wide read-only Dashboard for the shared viewer account, responsive on desktop and mobile. Admin actions and OCR interfaces are out of scope.

## Audience, job, proof, and constraints

Guild members need a fast collective overview without opening Members or Boss details. Lead with guild identity and captured guild facts: name, level, roster occupancy, total power, expedition points, and expedition tier. Show guild power history plus median member power, weekly donations, and guild boss damage. Use explicit dates for snapshot-derived information and never imply live data. English only; preserve existing routes and permissions.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Sober Archero guild command center: dark navy rail, cool light data canvas, cyan data accent, controlled guild gold, thin separators, minimal elevation, and exact existing boss art used only as a compact route cue. Approved composition: `.impeccable/mocks/dashboard-composition-f-shinigami-overview.png`.

The first viewport reads as the guild's identity card: Shinigami and its five defining statistics form one continuous band above a guild-power trajectory.

## Final composition override

Quick status contains only Roster and Latest boss result. The boss result is tied to the latest captured date and exact boss asset; it may show capture count and the leading recorded member. Do not show Today's boss, Last capture, live participation, or an empty Expedition placeholder.

## Unresolved decisions

Guild ID stays hidden from the primary band. Exact guild-power unit formatting must follow the eventual source contract. Expedition values may display an unavailable state until their ingestion contract exists.
